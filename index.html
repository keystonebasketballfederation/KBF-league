OFFICIAL RULES
CUBA is built around organized and fair competition.
01 — PLAYER ELIGIBILITY

All players must complete the official CUBA registration process before entering the player pool.

02 — DRAFT INTEGRITY

All draft selections must follow the official draft order and published league deadlines.

03 — GAME COMPLETION

Teams are responsible for completing scheduled games within the official league schedule.

04 — SPORTSMANSHIP

Players and GMs must respect opponents, teammates, league staff and the integrity of the competition.

05 — ROSTER MOVES

Free agents, replacements and roster changes must follow the official CUBA approval process.

06 — PLAYOFF QUALIFICATION

The top eight teams in the final regular-season standings qualify for the CUBA postseason.

07 — ADMINISTRATION

CUBA league administration has final authority regarding official league rulings, disputes and competitive decisions.
