LEAGUE STANDARDS

COMPETE THE RIGHT WAY.

KBF is built around competitive integrity, respect and organized league play.

Fair Play

  • Respect opponents and league staff.
  • Play all scheduled games competitively.
  • No exploiting or intentionally manipulating results.
  • Follow official league decisions and rulings.

Stay Locked In

  • Keep up with announcements and deadlines.
  • Communicate with opponents when scheduling games.
  • Report results accurately.
  • Stay active throughout the season.
