League Rules

Official CUBA competition standards

01 — DRAFT

Players are selected through the official CUBA draft.

02 — ROSTERS

Each team consists of one GM, seven drafted players and two free agents.

03 — REGULAR SEASON

Every team plays 16 regular-season games.

04 — PLAYOFFS

The top eight teams qualify for the postseason.

05 — RESULTS

Official game results must be reported and verified by league administration.

06 — CONDUCT

All players and GMs are expected to maintain competitive and respectful conduct.
